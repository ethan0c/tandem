# TANDEM — Complete Product Overview

# tandem

## Watch together. Read together. Stay connected.

A free, all-in-one anime streaming, manga reading, and social entertainment platform. Discover series, track your progress, import your existing libraries, and enjoy content together in real time.

Free to use

Mobile + Desktop

Anime + Manga

Tandem will expand from its existing watch-together website at ilovemygf.site into a complete anime and manga platform.

The goal is to combine the functionality of anime streaming sites, manga readers, tracking platforms, and synchronized watch-party apps into one seamless experience.

## 3. Accounts, profiles, and social features

Guest users can browse content and join or create public-link rooms without registering, while signed-in users receive persistent tracking and synchronization.

User profiles will include avatars, usernames, favorite anime and manga, reading/watching statistics, and optional public libraries.

Additional social features for later releases:

- Follow friends and view their public activity.
- Share manga and anime recommendations.
- Compare libraries and favorites.
- Invite friends directly into watch/read rooms.
- Optional activity privacy settings.

## 4. Notifications and automatic updates

Tandem will track the series users subscribe to and periodically check supported content APIs for new chapters and episodes.

Notifications can include new manga chapters, new anime episodes, updates to followed series, and shared-room invitations.

Users will be able to choose which notifications they receive. Email and push notifications can be added after the basic in-app notification system.

## 9. What makes Tandem different?

Most anime and manga platforms focus on one experience. Tandem would combine four:

1. Watch: Find and stream anime from supported sources.
2. Read: Read manga and track chapter progress.
3. Sync: Watch anime or read manga together in real time.
4. Import: Bring your existing manga library and progress from Weeb Central and eventually other platforms.

The biggest differentiators are watching/reading together and effortless library migration.

The overall product vision is a free, mobile-first anime and manga platform that lets people discover content, continue where they left off, and enjoy their favorite stories with someone else—without juggling multiple websites.

This is the proposed full feature scope, not a claim that all features already exist. I'd preserve Tandem's current room experience and build the new features around it rather than rewriting everything.

Then I'd build Tandem as a hybrid anime and manga platform.

Users should be able to watch or read directly inside Tandem whenever an authorized content source supports it. Otherwise, Tandem sends them to an external provider without losing their progress or subscriptions.

## How to minimize takedown risk

I'd keep Tandem's core application completely independent of its media integrations, use providers that explicitly allow embedding or reading, avoid proxying unauthorized streams, and implement a copyright complaint process.

This won't eliminate all legal or operational risk, but it is much more sustainable than building the platform around unlicensed streams.

One design decision I strongly recommend: Don't make users choose between two different websites. Give them one Tandem interface, with a clear Watch Here, Read Here, or Open Provider option depending on what is legitimately available.

That gives you the experience you're aiming for while keeping Tandem's libraries, social features, and watch-together functionality valuable even when a particular content source is unavailable.

### My proposed Tandem import feature

Users would be able to:

1. Copy their Weeb Central library and paste it into Tandem.
2. Automatically identify their manga subscriptions.
3. Restore last-read chapters where that information is available.
4. Match imported titles to Tandem's manga catalog.
5. Track new chapter releases and continue reading.

I'd also support XML, CSV, and text-file imports later, so the feature isn't limited to Weeb Central.

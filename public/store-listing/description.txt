Watch your favorite shows with someone who is far away.

Tandem keeps you watching the same moment and puts your video call beside the show. When one of you plays, pauses, or skips ahead, the other follows. No more counting down “3, 2, 1, play.”

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open the same episode on a supported site.
2. Click the Tandem button in your browser and choose “Create a room”.
3. Choose “Copy invite” and send the link to your partner.
4. Your partner opens the link with Tandem installed and joins your room.

FEATURES
• Watch together: when one of you pauses or skips, the other follows.
• Catch up automatically: if one video stops to load or falls behind, Tandem helps you get back to the same moment.
• Keep your call beside the show: see and hear each other in a small window. Move it anywhere or make it smaller.
• Keep talking in fullscreen: your call stays visible when you make the video fill the screen.
• Invite with one link: your partner gets set up, then lands on the same episode in your room.
• Plan watch nights: pick a time, and you each see it in your own time zone with a calendar reminder.
• Our shows: Tandem remembers where you stopped. Resume and you're both back at the same second.
• Send hearts and reactions that float across both screens.
• BRB: step away and the show waits for you.
• Ads don't split you up: on sites where Tandem can spot an ad, the other person's show waits until it's over.
• Join from your phone: watching on a TV? Keep the call on a phone.
• No account needed: choose a name and join.

SITES YOU CAN TRY
Tandem works on many popular streaming and video sites. If a site isn't supported yet, you can turn Tandem on for it from the Tandem button in your browser.

Some videos may not work yet. You both need access to the same episode, and ads can get you out of step.

PRIVACY
Tandem works on supported sites and additional sites you allow. It checks whether the video is playing or paused and where you are in it so you can watch together. Your list of shows stays in your browser and is never uploaded. Camera and microphone are used only for the call, after you allow them. Tandem does not read your browsing history, sell your information, or show ads. Read the full privacy details at https://www.ilovemygf.site/privacy.

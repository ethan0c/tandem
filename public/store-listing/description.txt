Watch anime with someone who isn't in the same room, and stay on the same frame the whole time.

Tandem keeps two video players in sync and puts your video call in a small floating window on top of the episode. When one of you presses play, pause or skips ahead, the other player does the same. You don't need to count down "3, 2, 1, play" again.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open an episode on a supported site.
2. Click the Tandem icon and press "Create a room".
3. Press "Copy invite" and send the link to your partner.
4. Your partner opens the link with Tandem installed and joins the same room at the same timestamp.

FEATURES
• Synced playback: play, pause and seek are mirrored on both screens.
• Automatic drift correction: if the two players slowly drift apart (buffering, slow connections), Tandem nudges them back together.
• Floating video call: see and hear each other in a small panel over the video. Drag it anywhere, or shrink it to just its title bar.
• Works in fullscreen: the player's own fullscreen button keeps the call visible on top.
• One-link invites: the invite is the episode URL with your room code added, so your partner lands on the right episode in the right room.
• No account needed: pick a display name and join.

SUPPORTED SITES
miruro.tv (and its .to / .online mirrors). More sites with standard HTML5 video players are planned.

PRIVACY
Tandem runs only on the supported sites listed above. It reads the state of the page's video player (playing or paused, current time) so it can sync it with your room. Camera and microphone are used only for the call, and only after you allow them. Tandem does not collect browsing history, does not sell data and does not show ads.

Made for long-distance watch nights. 💜

# Logo sources

Brand artwork is used to identify the services targeted by the extension. No partnership is implied.

- Netflix, YouTube, Max, Crunchyroll, Apple TV, Paramount+: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons (CC0 collection; brand rights remain with their owners).
- Prime Video: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/icons/primevideo.svg
- Hulu: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/9.21.0/icons/hulu.svg
- Disney+: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/disneyplus.svg (SVGL MIT collection).
- Peacock: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NBCUniversal_Peacock_Logo_(2026).svg (NBCUniversal; Commons labels the simple logo PD-textlogo).

Some Simple Icons SVGs have a brand-color fill added and their view boxes tightened to the artwork bounds. The asset sources do not establish playback compatibility.
